## Specification

- Colour: plum `#5B2C6F` on light backgrounds; light plum `#C39BD3` on dark
  backgrounds (8.1:1 contrast on GitHub's dark theme, where `#5B2C6F` is 1.8:1).
  The dark variants are the same artwork with only the colour changed.
- Tagline: "Command-line typing for *Caliciviridae*" (family name in italics,
  per ICTV convention)
- Wordmark: Inter SemiBold, letter-spacing −1.5
- Mark: hexagonal capsid silhouette with a knockout chalice. The cup is
  transparent, not white, so a single file works on light and dark backgrounds.
- The form refers to the family's etymology: *calicivirus*, from Latin *calix*,
  a cup, after the 32 cup-shaped depressions on the capsid surface.
